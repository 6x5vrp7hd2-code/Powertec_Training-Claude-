Programme

The 8-Stage Programme Structure

From entry competence to a formal recommendation for assessment — evidence-controlled, not time-controlled.

Stage 1

Entry Competence & Gap Analysis

Establish existing competence across HV operations, switching, proving dead and earthing, safety documents, protection, LV, UPS, generators, ATS/STS, commissioning, temporary supplies, control systems and data-centre experience. The result is an individual Training Needs Analysis.

Explore →
Stage 2

Understand the Architecture Before Operating It

The overlay method, formalised. The network is reconstructed progressively across 8 overlays rather than absorbed as one complete single-line diagram.

Explore →
Stage 3

Critical-Power Systems Modules

15 modules, each following the same structure: purpose, normal state, sources, alternative sources, failure modes, isolation implications, operational consequences, restoration.

Explore →
Stage 4

Safety Rules Conversion

Not a reproduction of another company's safety rules. What you already know, what changes here, why it changes — and the discipline of always asking “what can energise this equipment?” rather than “where is its normal supply.”

Explore →
Stage 5

Structured Practical Exposure

Shadowing with defined objectives before, during and after each observation — not open-ended time on site. Every operational activity becomes a training event without requiring the SAP to deliver a two-hour lesson.

Explore →
Stage 6

Progressive Competency Gates

8 formal gates. Failure at any gate identifies precisely what needs development — it doesn't send the trainee back to the beginning.

Explore →
Stage 7

Evidence Portfolio

A controlled portfolio of drawings, observed and assisted isolations, switching schedules, safety documents, commissioning activities, scenario assessments, knowledge checks and mentor reviews.

Explore →
Stage 8

Defined Development Timeline

A target readiness pathway, not a guaranteed authorisation date. Someone exceptional can progress faster. Someone who hasn't demonstrated competence doesn't progress simply because time has elapsed.

Explore →

Principle

Time served should not equal competence. Progression through the eight gates is controlled by demonstrated evidence, not weeks on site.
