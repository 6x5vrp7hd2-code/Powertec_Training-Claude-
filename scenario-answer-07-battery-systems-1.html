Scenario

A battery string has been disconnected from the DC bus for scheduled maintenance. Is it now safe to work on? What hazard remains regardless?

Model Answer

Not automatically safe — the string itself remains a charged source at its own terminals even when disconnected from the bus. Arc-flash and shock hazard remains until the string is proven dead cell-by-cell or otherwise confirmed safe per manufacturer procedure.

This is a model answer, not the only acceptable one — if your reasoning reaches the same operational conclusion by a different route, discuss it with a mentor rather than treating this as the sole correct wording.
