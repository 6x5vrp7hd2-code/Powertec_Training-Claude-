Section 06

Stored Energy — Capacitors and Batteries

The guide’s central point — that opening all AC sources does not make a UPS “dead” — is one of the most frequently cited root causes in electrical incident investigations involving power electronics equipment, and it is why lock-out/tag-out standards worldwide (NFPA 70E, EN 50110, NF C 18-510) all treat capacitor-bus and battery isolation as a mandatory, separate step from AC source isolation, never assumed to be covered by it.

Additional context:

Capacitor stored energy in real terms. The formula E = ½CU² means stored energy scales with the square of voltage: doubling the DC bus voltage quadruples the stored energy for the same capacitance. Modern UPS DC buses commonly run at 400–800 V, and with tens of millifarads of bulk capacitance, the stored energy genuinely reaches kilojoule levels — for context, a few kilojoules delivered in a short arc is comparable to the energy in a small hand grenade’s blast, which is why manufacturer discharge times (5–10 minutes) and a mandatory voltmeter check (never a “short it and see” approach) are treated as non-negotiable.

Lithium-ion thermal runaway. As lithium-ion batteries increasingly replace VRLA (valve-regulated lead-acid) batteries in UPS applications, the guide’s brief mention of “lithium batteries add the risk of thermal runaway” reflects a well-documented failure mode: an internal short or overcharge can trigger an exothermic chemical reaction that self-sustains and escalates, potentially leading to fire or explosion, and cannot be extinguished by simply removing electrical power — this is why lithium battery rooms in data centres increasingly require dedicated fire suppression and gas-detection systems distinct from those protecting VRLA battery rooms.

Bleeder resistors and IEC 62040 (UPS standard). IEC 62040-1 (UPS safety requirements) specifies discharge-time requirements for capacitors to reach safe touch- voltage levels after de-energisation, which is the standards basis for the manufacturer- specified 5–10 minute discharge windows the guide describes.
