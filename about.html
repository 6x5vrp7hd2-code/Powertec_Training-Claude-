About the Programme

Why Powertec Training exists, and the philosophy behind how it's built.

Why this exists

DNO SAP work is predominantly HV-orientated. The permit and safe system of work reflects this — a permit to work is only issued in the HV network, because that activity requires the HV circuit to be DIESR: Dead, Isolated, Earthed, Screened, Recorded. The LV network, while dangerous, is a single system.

Data centres are the reverse. The LV system is phenomenally complex — UPS, AHU, ATS, load bank testing, full interconnection design with multiple infeeds to almost every item of plant — multiplied across every cell and every colo on site.

Authorised Persons and Senior Authorised Persons on site are already fully occupied providing isolations, earthing, issuing safety documents and supervising commissioning teams. They rarely have time to properly coach a new AP/SAP through systems they already understand back to front — and when they do try, the depth of their own knowledge can overwhelm a trainee who has plenty of SAP experience, just not in this particular arena.

The learning philosophy

This programme is built on a specific personal learning method: shadow, observe, digest. Draw your own diagrams. Strip the entire system down to overlays. Rebuild it back up, piece by piece.

It gives a trainee structure, deadlines, benchmarks and completeness — with a practical and achievable path to assessment, rather than an open-ended period of "picking things up."

Two pathways

Experienced SAP → Data-Centre SAP conversion. Acknowledges existing HV competence rather than re-teaching electrical safety from first principles. Focuses on the systems-knowledge and operational-context gap specifically.

CP/AP → Data-Centre SAP development. A fuller development path for engineers building toward SAP for the first time within a data-centre environment.
