Scenario Card 1 — Model Answer

Scenario

You isolate the primary infeed to a PDU. A secondary infeed remains connected via an ATS in standby. Is the PDU safe to work on? What would make it safe?

Model Answer

Not safe as described — the standby infeed remains live and capable of energising the PDU via the ATS, whether by automatic transfer or fault condition. It becomes safe only once the secondary infeed is also isolated, proven dead, and the ATS itself is confirmed isolated or physically prevented from transferring.

This is a model answer, not the only acceptable one — if your reasoning reaches the same operational conclusion by a different route, discuss it with a mentor rather than treating this as the sole correct wording.
