Interlocking

The physical enforcement layer that prevents an unsafe switching sequence from being physically possible, not just procedurally forbidden.

LessonOverviewSources & Alternative SourcesFailure Modes & IsolationConsequences & RestorationTraining StrategyKnowledge Bank

Failure Modes & Isolation Implications

A defeated or bypassed interlock, whether through a duplicated key, a maintenance override, or physical damage, removes the physical enforcement entirely and returns the site to relying on procedure alone. This should be treated as a significant elevation of risk, not a minor deviation.
