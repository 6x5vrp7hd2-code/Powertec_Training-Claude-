Scenario Card 3 — Model Answer

Scenario

Following restoration, the BMS/DCIM still shows a piece of plant as isolated even though it has been re-energised. What does this discrepancy mean for closing out the permit?

Model Answer

The permit should not be closed out until the discrepancy is resolved — either the monitoring point needs correcting, or there's a genuine mismatch between actual and intended plant state that needs investigating before anyone relies on the BMS reading again.

This is a model answer, not the only acceptable one — if your reasoning reaches the same operational conclusion by a different route, discuss it with a mentor rather than treating this as the sole correct wording.
