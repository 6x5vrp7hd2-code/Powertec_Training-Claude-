ATS / STS and Alternate Supplies

The mechanism that actually executes the resilient-path concept, where transfer speed and failure mode both matter operationally.

Purpose

The mechanism that actually executes the resilient-path concept from Module 4 — and the point where transfer speed, transfer method, and failure mode all matter operationally.

Normal State

An Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) uses electromechanical contactors or motorised breakers to switch a load between two sources, with a brief interruption typically in the 50–100ms range. A Static Transfer Switch (STS) uses solid-state SCR/thyristor switching to transfer within a fraction of a cycle, roughly 4–8ms, fast enough to be effectively seamless to IT load — which is why STS is the standard choice for dual-corded critical loads, while ATS remains common for generator-side and less transfer-sensitive applications.
