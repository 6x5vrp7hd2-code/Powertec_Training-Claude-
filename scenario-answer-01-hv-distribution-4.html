Scenario

A permit to work has been issued for HV switchgear maintenance. Partway through, the standby feed automatically closes onto the bus due to a control fault. What should have prevented this, and what is the immediate priority?

Model Answer

Immediate priority is protecting anyone working under the permit — treat the bus as potentially live immediately, withdraw personnel to a safe position, and re-prove dead before continuing. The root cause — why the auto-close logic wasn't inhibited as part of permit preparation — must be investigated before switching resumes.

This is a model answer, not the only acceptable one — if your reasoning reaches the same operational conclusion by a different route, discuss it with a mentor rather than treating this as the sole correct wording.
