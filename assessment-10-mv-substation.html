Assessment · Section 10

The MV Substation and Its Switchgear

Work through the multiple-choice and long-answer questions, then check your answers.

Multiple Choice

1. What is a Ring Main Unit (RMU)?

2. What is the purpose of the VPIS (Voltage Presence Indicating System)?

3. What does DGPT2 (or the older Buchholz relay) detect on an oil-immersed transformer?

4. What is the key difference between a DM1 (circuit breaker) and QM/PM (switch-fuse combination) transformer protection cubicle?

5. Why does the metering cubicle (CM/GBC) have no switching device?

Long Answer

1. Explain why the VPIS is described as “an indication, never a proof,” and describe what must always be done in addition before working on a cubicle.

2. Compare the DM1 (circuit breaker) and QM/PM (switch-fuse) transformer protection cubicle variants, including when each is typically used and how each is identified on a diagram and in the field.

3. Describe the function and significance of the bus tie cubicle between two half-busbars, and explain the “2 keys for 3 breakers” interlock referenced in relation to it.

4. Explain why the metering cubicle is treated differently from every other cubicle in the substation in terms of who may work on it.

5. Using the cubicle summary table’s logic, explain how a specialist would identify, purely from visual inspection in the field (without a diagram), which type of cubicle they are looking at.

Finished? Check your answers.
