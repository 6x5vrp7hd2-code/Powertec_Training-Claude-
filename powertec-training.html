About the programme, why it exists, and what it's built to produce.

About

Powertec Training is a division of Powertec, sitting alongside Powertec Utilities. It exists to convert experienced electrical operational personnel — predominantly DNO-background HV Authorised and Senior Authorised Persons — into the data-centre and critical-environments world, and to develop CP/APs toward Data-Centre SAP for the first time.

Purpose

An experienced SAP arriving on a data-centre site already knows how to work safely. What they don't yet know is this specific environment's systems, its interconnection design, and the operational judgement that environment demands. This programme closes that specific gap — deliberately, on a defined timeline, without re-teaching electrical safety fundamentals the trainee already holds.

The fundamental skills gap: DNO vs. critical environments

DNO SAP work is predominantly HV-orientated. A permit to work is only issued in the HV network, because that activity requires the HV circuit to be DIESR — Dead, Isolated, Earthed, Screened, Recorded. The LV network, while dangerous, is a single system: you're either maintaining a customer supply or running an outage.

Data centres invert this. The LV system is phenomenally complex — UPS, AHU, ATS, load bank testing, full interconnection design with multiple infeeds to almost every item of plant — multiplied across every cell and every colo on site. The SAP mindset transfers; the systems knowledge does not, and that mismatch is exactly what this programme is built to close.

Pathway options

Which pathway applies is determined by the Skills Gap Analysis, not assumed in advance.

Pathway A

Experienced SAP → Data-Centre SAP Conversion

For engineers who already hold HV SAP authorisation elsewhere. Acknowledges existing competence rather than re-teaching electrical safety from first principles; focuses on systems knowledge and operational context specific to critical environments.

Pathway B

CP/AP → Data-Centre SAP Development

For engineers building toward SAP for the first time within a data-centre environment. A fuller development path covering both the underlying electrical principles and the critical-environments systems layer together.

Required outcome

The programme's stated outcome is straightforward: the appropriate level of authorisation, reached in quick succession, based on demonstrated learning outcomes and the individual's own skills-gap analysis — not on time elapsed on site. See The Platform for how progress toward that outcome is structured and tracked.
