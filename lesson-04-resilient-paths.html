Lesson · Module 04

Resilient Paths

Read this in full before attempting the scenarios below — they assume this material.

A and B are a philosophy, not just two cables

The single biggest mental shift for a DNO-trained SAP is treating 'redundant' as something to verify, not something to assume from the presence of two cables. Two paths that both trace back to the same upstream transformer are not redundant in any meaningful sense — they share the one failure that matters. Genuine redundancy means independence all the way back to source, and confirming that independence is a specific, checkable thing, not a design intention taken on faith.

Tier III and Tier IV — a real operational difference, not just a rating

The Uptime Institute's Tier system isn't a marketing label; it describes a specific operational guarantee. Tier III gives concurrent maintainability — one path can be worked on without dropping load, but the site isn't proven to survive an unplanned failure of the other path during that maintenance window. Tier IV requires the site to survive an unplanned failure on either path, including while the other is under maintenance. Knowing which one applies to the cell you're working in changes what 'safe to isolate one path' actually means in practice.

Capacity is not automatic

A path that is electrically present and independent still isn't 'redundant' in a useful sense if it lacks the spare capacity to carry the full load. An automatic transfer that succeeds electrically but overloads the receiving path produces the exact outage the redundancy was meant to prevent. Capacity has to be confirmed by calculation or by test, not assumed from the fact that a transfer path exists on the SLD.

What 'fully restored' means for a resilient path

After maintenance on one path, restoring 'A+B running' isn't complete the moment both paths are reconnected. It's complete once both paths have been independently proven capable of carrying full load. This distinction — reconnected versus proven — is exactly what the scenarios for this module are testing.
