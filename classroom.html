Two strands, learned together: 21 key theory and principle modules, and 15 scenario-training modules that test judgement, not recall.

Strand 1 · 21 modules

Fundamental & Key Theory and Principles

21 lesson modules covering the electrical fundamentals and critical-power principles this programme is built on — from basic electrical quantities through to the Red List of mistakes never to make. Each lesson ends with a multiple-choice and long-answer assessment, then an answer key.

Strand 2 · 15 modules

Scenario Training

15 modules, each with a full lesson specific to this site's systems, followed by 4 functional-testing scenarios and model answers. This strand applies the theory from Strand 1 to real isolation-planning judgement.

How the two strands relate

Strand 1 builds the underlying electrical and critical-power knowledge in a fixed, general sequence. Strand 2 applies that knowledge to this site's actual systems and tests it with realistic isolation- planning scenarios. Working through both, in parallel with the Skills Gap Analysis and the Training Dashboard, is what the programme's evidence portfolio is built from.
