Scenario Card 2 — Model Answer

Compare your reasoning against this model answer, then discuss any gaps with a mentor.

Scenario

You need to isolate one incomer on a dual-fed LV board without dropping the board. Explain the switching sequence, including the bus-tie.

Model Answer

With the bus-tie closed and both incomers in service, first confirm the adjacent section can carry the combined load. Open and lock off the incomer to be isolated, then prove dead and earth that incomer's cable and breaker; the board remains live throughout via the bus-tie from the other incomer.

This is a model answer, not the only acceptable one — if your reasoning reaches the same operational conclusion by a different route, discuss it with a mentor rather than treating this as the sole correct wording.
