Multiple Infeeds / Backfeeds

Where the interconnection design multiplies the risk that HV-trained thinking underestimates.

LessonOverviewSources & Alternative SourcesFailure Modes & IsolationConsequences & RestorationTraining StrategyKnowledge Bank

Purpose

Where the interconnection design multiplies the risk that HV-trained thinking underestimates: a load fed from “one supply” in a DNO sense may in a data centre be fed, in sequence or in parallel, from several sources that are not obvious from a single board.

Normal State

Multiple infeed points converge on shared downstream equipment via ATS, STS, or bus-tie arrangements.
