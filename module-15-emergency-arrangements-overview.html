Emergency Arrangements

The last-resort layer, deliberately simple in design intent, and exactly where a poorly understood activation causes expensive, self-inflicted outages.

Purpose

The last-resort layer, deliberately simple in design intent — but exactly where an accidental or poorly understood activation causes some of the data centre industry's most expensive, entirely self-inflicted outages.

Normal State

An Emergency Power Off (EPO) system, where fitted, is designed to remove power from IT and electronic equipment in the room in a genuine emergency, distinct from routine or even urgent-but-planned shutdown. In UK and EU data centres, EPO is not always a code requirement in the way it historically has been under the US National Electrical Code — site-specific design intent should be confirmed rather than assumed.
