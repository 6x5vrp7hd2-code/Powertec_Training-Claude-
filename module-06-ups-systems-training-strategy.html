UPS Systems

The single largest knowledge gap for a DNO-trained SAP: multiple simultaneous internal power paths with no HV switching equivalent.

LessonOverviewSources & Alternative SourcesFailure Modes & IsolationConsequences & RestorationTraining StrategyKnowledge Bank

Overlay Target

Diagram normal, static bypass, and maintenance-bypass paths for one UPS module, marking every isolation point.

Shadowing Objective

Practical Observation — UPS isolation. Before attending: identify the normal supply, the bypass source, the battery/DC energy path, and downstream implications from the SLD. During: observe the switching sequence, isolation points, proving-dead requirements, and document issue. Afterwards: draw the final isolated configuration and explain every possible backfeed route.

Scenario Card

Scenario

UPS A requires maintenance. Normal input remains healthy. Battery string remains connected. Static bypass source is available. Downstream load cannot be interrupted. Identify the operational state required before work begins and every potential source of energy that must be considered.
