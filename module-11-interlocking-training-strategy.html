Interlocking

The physical enforcement layer that prevents an unsafe switching sequence from being physically possible, not just procedurally forbidden.

Overlay Target

Diagram one interlocked switching sequence, such as a bus-tie/incomer scheme, and the specific dangerous configurations the interlock exists to prevent.

Shadowing Objective

Observe a key-exchange sequence in an interlocked switchboard. Record the order of operations and confirm which unsafe configuration each step rules out.
