Assessment · Section 06

Stored Energy — Capacitors and Batteries

Work through the multiple-choice and long-answer questions, then check your answers.

Multiple Choice

1. What is the formula for energy stored in a capacitor?

2. Why is a capacitor’s discharge time deliberately slow (several minutes)?

3. Why does a battery remain dangerous even after all AC sources to a UPS are opened and locked?

4. What is the only way to fully isolate a UPS battery?

5. What additional hazard do lithium-ion batteries introduce compared to VRLA batteries?

Long Answer

1. Explain why the manufacturer’s discharge time is described as “a necessary condition, not a proof,” and describe the correct verification procedure that follows it.

2. Using the formula E = ½CU², explain why doubling the DC bus voltage has a much larger effect on stored energy than doubling the capacitance, and why this matters for UPS safety.

3. Describe, step by step, why “opening all the AC inputs of a UPS” does not make the battery circuit safe, and what must be done in addition.

4. Explain why shorting a charged capacitor with an uninsulated tool is dangerous, and what the correct alternative is if a discharge tool is needed.

5. Compare the specific risks (voltage, arc behaviour, failure mode) of a UPS’s capacitor bus versus its battery string.

Finished? Check your answers.
