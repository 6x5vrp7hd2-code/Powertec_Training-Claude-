Protection

Where discrimination between protective devices determines whether a fault takes down one final circuit or cascades through an entire board.

OverviewSources & Alternative SourcesFailure Modes & IsolationConsequences & RestorationTraining StrategyKnowledge Bank

Purpose

Where discrimination, or selectivity, between protective devices determines whether a fault takes down one final circuit or cascades up through an entire board — a concept every SAP knows in principle, but data centre-specific discrimination studies are considerably more layered than a typical DNO network.

Normal State

Downstream devices should clear a fault before upstream devices operate: a feeder breaker trips before the main, a panel breaker trips before the board feeder supplying it. This is formalised in standards such as BS 7671 and equivalent IEC guidance, typically expressed as minimum current or time ratios between series-connected devices.
