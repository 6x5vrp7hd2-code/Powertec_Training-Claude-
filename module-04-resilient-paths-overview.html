Resilient Paths

The A/B supply philosophy that makes data centre LV fundamentally different from a single-system DNO network.

Purpose

The A/B supply philosophy that makes data centre LV fundamentally different from a single-system DNO network — this is the concept that most needs unlearning and relearning, not just adding to.

Normal State

A/B supplies run in parallel from separate upstream sources to dual-corded loads. Bus couplers and alternate feeds exist to reconfigure the path without dropping load.
