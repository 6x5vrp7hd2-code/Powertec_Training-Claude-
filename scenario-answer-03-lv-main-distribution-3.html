Scenario

A board is split with the bus-tie open, each section fed independently. One section trips. What determines whether the other section is affected, and what would you check before closing the bus-tie?

Model Answer

Whether the other section is affected depends entirely on whether the bus-tie is genuinely open and proven so — if it is, the healthy section is unaffected. Before closing the bus-tie to restore the tripped section, confirm the fault has actually been found and cleared, not just that the breaker has reset.

This is a model answer, not the only acceptable one — if your reasoning reaches the same operational conclusion by a different route, discuss it with a mentor rather than treating this as the sole correct wording.
