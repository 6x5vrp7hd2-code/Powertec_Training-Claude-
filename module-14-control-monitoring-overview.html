Purpose

The layer that gives visibility across everything else in this programme — and the layer most likely to be dismissed as “someone else's problem” by an engineer focused purely on the electrical plant.

Normal State

A Building Management System (BMS) typically covers facility-wide control: power, environmental monitoring, HVAC, lighting, access control, and fire alarm integration. Data Centre Infrastructure Management (DCIM) sits alongside or above it, focused specifically on mission-critical infrastructure: asset tracking, capacity planning, thermal mapping, and detailed electrical and environmental monitoring purpose-built for data centre operations.
