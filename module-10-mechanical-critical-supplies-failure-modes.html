Mechanical Critical Supplies (AHU / CRAC)

The boundary where electrical isolation meets mechanical plant, and where responsibility can become genuinely ambiguous without a clear handover point.

Failure Modes & Isolation Implications

Electrically isolating a unit does not remove control-side interlocks, and mechanical shutdown of a unit does not remove electrical supply. These are two separate isolations that both need to be planned and both need to be proven, with a clearly defined point at which electrical permit responsibility ends and mechanical permit responsibility begins.
