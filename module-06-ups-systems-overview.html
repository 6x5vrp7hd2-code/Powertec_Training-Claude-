UPS Systems

The single largest knowledge gap for a DNO-trained SAP: multiple simultaneous internal power paths with no HV switching equivalent.

Purpose

The single largest knowledge gap for a DNO-trained SAP: HV switching has no equivalent to a UPS's multiple simultaneous internal power paths.

Normal State

In the standard double-conversion topology, the rectifier converts incoming AC to DC, continuously charging the battery and supplying the inverter. The inverter converts DC back to clean AC for the load. Because power is continuously converted rather than switched in on failure, there is no transfer time during a mains-loss event — the battery simply takes up the DC bus duty the rectifier was providing.
