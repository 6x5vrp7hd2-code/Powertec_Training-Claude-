Failure Modes & Isolation Implications

A plant item correctly isolated at the switchboard can still show “healthy” on the BMS or DCIM if the monitoring point itself has failed or lost communication. Never rely on a monitoring system as the sole confirmation of an isolation; always verify at the plant.
